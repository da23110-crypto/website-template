Maunga Club - Kakapo Chalet

The Kakapo chalet is a cozy and comfortable accommodation option for visitors to the Maunga Club. It features a warm and inviting interior, with a spacious living area, fully equipped kitchen, and comfortable bedrooms. The chalet is perfect for families or groups of friends looking for a relaxing getaway in the beautiful surroundings of the Maunga Club.

The biggest of the three chalets, the kakapo chalet can accommodate a maximum of 10 adults and up to 30 children.

Exterior: The kakapo chalet is a large and welcoming accommodation option set in the heart of the forest, offering stunning views of the surrounding natural landscape.

Inside the chalet: The interior of the kakapo chalet is designed to provide a comfortable and cozy atmosphere for all guests.

Bunk room: The bunk room is a spacious area designed for the comfort and entertainment of the children.

Views: Visitors can observe the beautiful natural surroundings from the windows of the kakapo chalet.
