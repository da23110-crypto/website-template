Welcome to Maunga Club, a place for all your outdoor adventures!
Perfect for weekend getaways, all bookings run
from Friday to Sunday, providing shared accommodation for groups ranging from
6 to 40 people per chalet. With our three chalets situated right on the slopes, members
enjoy seamless ski-in and ski-out access to 550 hectares of terrain and New Zealand's longest ski and
snowboard season. Join the Maunga Club today to gain access to our
mountain cabins and experience the ultimate snow getaway!

We offer three different chalets, each with its own unique features and amenities.
Our chalets are designed to provide a comfortable and cozy environment for our members, ensuring
a memorable stay in the mountains. Whether you're looking for a relaxing retreat
or an action-packed adventure, Maunga Club has something for everyone.
