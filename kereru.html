Maunga Club - Kereru Chalet

The Kereru chalet is a spacious and comfortable accommodation option for visitors to the Maunga Club. It features a warm and inviting interior, with a large living area, fully equipped kitchen, and comfortable bedrooms. The chalet is perfect for families or groups of friends looking for a relaxing getaway in the beautiful surroundings of the Maunga Club.

The smallest of the three chalets, the Kereru chalet can accommodate 2 adults and 4 children.

Exterior: The kereru chalet is a small but cozy accommodation option set lakeside with beautiful views of the snowy landscape around the lake.

Adult bedroom: The adult bedroom is a comfortable space designed for relaxation seperate from the children's sleeping area.

Kids' bedroom: The kids' bedrooms are a fun and cozy space designed for the children's comfort and entertainment.

Kitchen: The rustic look of this kitchen sets the vibe of the room. Boasting a fully equipped kitchen with all necessary amenities for preparing meals for the entire family.
