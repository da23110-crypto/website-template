Maunga Club - Pukeko Chalet

The Pukeko chalet is a charming and inviting accommodation option for visitors to the Maunga Club. It features a warm and welcoming interior, with a spacious living area, fully equipped kitchen, and comfortable bedrooms. The chalet is perfect for families or groups of friends looking for a relaxing getaway in the beautiful surroundings of the Maunga Club.

The Pukeko chalet is a medium sized chalet that can accommodate up to 6 adults and 15 children.

Exterior: The pukeko chalet is a charming and inviting accommodation option set in the heart of the Maunga Club, offering stunning views of the surrounding landscape.

Adult bedroom: The adult bedroom is a comfortable space designed for relaxation nestled under the open rafters of the roof.

Chalet view: Visitors can enjoy the scenic views from the windows of the pukeko chalet.

Bathroom: The bathroom is a clean and well-equipped space for the comfort of all guests.
